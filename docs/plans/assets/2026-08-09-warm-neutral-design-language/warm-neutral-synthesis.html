Warmth lives in large surfaces

The canvas and panels use warm gray and ivory. Text and controls stay neutral, so the product feels humane without becoming “earth-toned.”

Contrast carries hierarchy

Near-black primary actions, strong type hierarchy, discrete active fills, and sparse elevation make states legible without hard outlines.

Color has a job

Blue marks selection and focus; annotation hues identify type; green confirms safe success. Routine chrome remains neutral.

Explicitly avoid

No green wash, capsule-everything styling, hard black outlines, decorative gradients, glass effects, or shadow on every surface.
